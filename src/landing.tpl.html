Make a lower third for an edit

Six steps from a blank design to a file your editor can use.

Video Lower Thirds37 s
  1. Type the text. Name, title and school. Long text shrinks to fit.
  2. Pick a style. Seven layouts, all on brand.
  3. Set the look. Plate color, accent, logo, your own graphic.
  4. Choose the motion. In and out animation, then press Play.
  5. Add a rundown if you have many names. Paste them from Google Sheets.
  6. Export. Pick the format that fits your editor.

Which format? Final Cut Pro and Premiere Pro take the MOV with alpha. CapCut and web video take the WebM with alpha. If you are unsure, the PNG sequence works in every editor.

Run lower thirds on a live stream

Built for parades, games and events where names, sponsors and intermission cards come up on cue.

Live Lower Thirds43 s
  1. Design your look in the same tool: style, color and type.
  2. Add your rundown. Paste from Google Sheets or upload a CSV.
  3. Open the output window. A solid green screen with your graphic.
  4. In OBS, add it as a Window Capture, then add a Chroma Key filter.
  5. Press TAKE on a row to go on air. CLEAR takes it off.
  6. Use the keys. Space takes the next row, Esc clears, arrows move the cue.

Good to know. Keep the output window uncovered, or in full screen, so it keeps animating. Do not use green in your graphics, since green is what OBS removes.

GUIDE

Which one do I need?

If the graphic ends up inside a finished video, use the video tool. If it appears while people are watching, use the live tool.
