OBS on a Mac, step by step
  1. In OBS, under Sources press the + button and choose Window Capture. Pick the window named OUHSD Live Output.
  2. Right-click the new source, choose Filters, press + under Effect Filters and choose Chroma Key. Set Key Color Type to Green (or the color you picked above).
  3. Turn Similarity up to about 400 and Smoothness to about 80 if you see a green edge. Spill Reduction around 100 removes green fringing.
  4. Drag the source so it covers the whole canvas. The output window keeps a 16 by 9 shape inside, so it lines up with your video.
  5. Keep the output window uncovered. Chrome pauses animation in a window that is minimized or fully hidden behind other windows, so put it on a second display, or press F for full screen on its own desktop space.
  6. Do not use the key color in your graphics. The district colors are safe with green or magenta keys.

Rundown

One row per lower third or title card, in the order you will run them. Paste a table from Google Sheets or Excel (Name, Title, Third line, optional Style), or upload a CSV. Click a number to show that row in the preview, then edit it here or in the Content tab.

Export all uses the format selected in Export above and saves one zip, one folder per row, numbered in this order.

Click TAKE on a row to put it on air. The row marked ON AIR is what viewers see. Add, reorder or edit rows any time, even while live.
